The tutor at the desk.

Turn Innernet AI on and everything works: your PDFs come in cleaned, cited and tagged, and every passage you highlight can be talked through with a tutor that has done its reading first.

Clean, cited highlights

Text repair, tags, citations and page numbers for every PDF you add. The engine never formats from thin air — it works out the source's details and the app builds each note in your chosen style.

Chat about this

A tutor that researches each highlight — the text around it, the source's opening pages, real background from the web — then talks it through with you, toward your own title and your own notes.

In the connections, never instead of you

On an insight's board the tutor never suggests a connection. It asks the questions, a turn at a time, and your own words land as your comments.

Unlimited, with no keys

No servers, no API keys, no models to set up. Sign in inside the app and it all works; cancel any time.

How a conversation goes

You send what's on the card. The tutor opens on what it has read, keeps to the passage, writes in plain words, and asks before it strays — whatever it brings in from outside the highlight, it gives context for first. When a conversation stalls, a senior tutor reads it over and sets the next few turns a brief. You keep writing the title and the notes; the tutor is there to get you to them.

Join the beta

Innernet AI isn't on sale yet. While the app is in beta, everything in it is open — leave your email and you'll be in when it is.

Your reading stays yours

Your PDFs, highlights and notes are read on your machine, and the tutor's web lookups run there too. What passes through our relay is only what the feature needs — the passage in, the reply out — and your messages are not used to train models.
