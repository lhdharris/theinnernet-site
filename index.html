A home for your highlights.

Innernet is a reader for the PDFs you keep coming back to. Highlight what matters, watch those highlights gather into a note field of linked thoughts, and let optional AI help you read harder — cleanup, tags, citations, and a tutor that keeps you inside the text.

Read closely

Open a PDF and mark it up. Highlights stay attached to the page and to each other, in one quiet field of notes rather than a folder of loose quotes.

Think in the open

Incomplete highlights, insights, and the note field show your reading as it grows — so a long book becomes a map you can walk.

Ask the text

With Innernet AI on, the tutor keeps to the passage it's given — first what the author is saying, then why it matters — and web lookups stay on your machine.

Own your files

Your PDFs live on your computer. Deleting all data is one setting, done in the app.
